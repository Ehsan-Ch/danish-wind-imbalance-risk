Scale a hypothetical share of zone wind generation. Q10 nominates a lower volume, Q50 the median, and Q90 a higher volume. Each is an alternative scenario for the same delivery hours.

Historical effect = (actual − nominated) × (imbalance price − day-ahead price). Positive is a benefit relative to selling actual output at day-ahead prices. Daily tail loss is the mean of the worst 5% of complete-day losses, rounded up to whole days.

Cumulative historical imbalance effect in EUR: Q10 green · Q50 orange · Q90 blue. Missing priced hours are excluded; partial days remain in this plot.

These are counterfactual nomination scenarios, not executable day-ahead bids. Lead times are relative to delivery, not auction deadlines. Hourly generation is assumed flat within each hour. Stress charges are hypothetical penalties, separate from historical settlement prices; they do not alter the historical-effect column.
